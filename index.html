Why Thinker exists

Thinker started as a hobby project, back when tools like Notion and Milanote, as good as they are, charged a lot for something that should be simple. I built Thinker to help creative folks like you and me brainstorm, stay organized, and track progress, all at a price that makes sense.

Thinker will always be free for people who need it but can't afford it. I use the sales to build Thinker Cloud and to fund my film projects.
